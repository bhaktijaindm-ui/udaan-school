FAQ Overview

What are school timings?

Nursery & KG: 8:30 AM - 12:30 PM.
Primary (Class 1-5): 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM.

Do you offer after-school care?

Yes, we have an extension program until 5:00 PM for working parents.

Can I book a weekend tour?

Yes, we offer specialized school tours on Saturdays with prior appointment.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

Our primary classrooms maintain a strict 15:1 ratio to ensure highly personalized attention for every child across subjects.

Are meals provided on campus?

Yes! We have a purely organic, nutritionist-designed cafeteria providing hot, fresh meals with zero processed sugars.
