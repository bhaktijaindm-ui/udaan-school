Meet Our Teachers: Heart of Udaaan

February 25, 2024 By Udaaan Community 7 Min Read

At Udaaan School, the architecture and the resources are secondary to the humans who interact with your children daily. Today, we sit down with Ms. Geeta Menon, our Head of Early Curriculums.

Ms. Menon holds a Master's degree in Early Childhood Psychology and has spent over 15 years developing empathetic pedagogies across leading Indian institutions before finding her permanent home at Udaaan.

1. The Interview

Admin: "Geeta, what drew you to Udaaan School initially?"

Geeta: "The absolute lack of standardized testing for nursery and KG students here. In my previous institutions, children were being judged by rote memorization at age 4. Udaaan was the first place that let me teach through play, strictly."

2. Teaching Philosophy

Admin: "How do you handle a disruptive classroom?"

Geeta: "By understanding that a 'disruptive' classroom is just an 'under-stimulated' classroom. If they are running around screaming, it means my lesson on sitting still is inherently deeply flawed for their current biological energy levels. I immediately shift the lesson to incorporate physical movement."

3. Message to Parents

Admin: "What is your core advice for new Udaaan parents?"

Geeta: "Trust the process. Your child may come home covered in paint without knowing their alphabets yet. But I promise you, they spent their day developing fine motor skills, sharing supplies with peers, and developing a permanent, passionate love for the concept of learning. The alphabet will easily follow when they are ready."

4. Contact the Faculty

Ms. Geeta is available for Parent-Teacher consultations globally during the first Friday of every month. You may book appointments via the school app.
