Parent Answers

Everything You Need To Know.

Parenting is an incredible journey, and picking the right educational environment is everything. We've compiled the top 10 most critical questions our community of mothers and fathers ask us.

Still have doubts?

Our admission counselors are ready to help chart your child's growth map.

Contact Counselor
What is the exact age criteria for Nursery?
For the session 2024-25, the child must be born between April 2020 and March 2021 as per the national mandate. We heavily focus on developmental readiness over strict age cutoffs when evaluating.
Do you provide completely secure transport?
Absolutely! Our modern fleet of AC buses features live GPS-tracking sent directly to your phone, female attendants permanently on board, and strict speed-governing locks covering major Gurgaon sectors.
What is the teacher-student classroom ratio?
We maintain a strict 1:15 ratio with one lead mentor and one assistant teacher in every room. This avoids crowd-teaching and allows for deep, personal mentoring and constant emotional supervision.
How do you manage device screen time?
Technology is purely a tool here, not a babysitter. 80% of our day relies on kinetic, hands-on learning outside. The 20% dedicated to tablets uses exclusive logic-building apps in heavily monitored short intervals.
How do you handle severe separation anxiety?
The first two weeks allow mothers to sit just outside the learning pods. Our psychologists utilize specific transitional play therapy to ease your child slowly into forming independent trust with their mentor.
Are daily meals provided and nutritious?
Yes. We have an in-house organic kitchen curated by a pediatric nutritionist. All meals are purely vegetarian, hot, varied daily, and free of any processed refined sugars or generic artificial flavors.
What is your core approach to discipline?
We reject harsh or traditional punishments. We use 'positive reinforcement' and 'restorative reflection corners'. We guide children to comprehend WHY an action was wrong, building intrinsic moral compasses instead of fear.
How heavily involved can parents be?
Extremely! We have monthly "Parent Coffee Mornings", "Hero Reading Days" where you read to the class, and a dedicated mobile app that serves you real-time picture updates of their daily milestones while you are at work.
How is a child's academic progress tracked?
There are zero traditional written exams in the primary blocks. We generate deep "Milestone Portfolios" based on observations during play, puzzle-solving speeds, and artistic expressions to gauge cognitive growth.
Do you have an after-school care program?
For working parents, our "Sunset Hub" runs until 5:30 PM. It is not just babysitting daycare; the children participate in independent reading, instrumental music access, and calm wind-down yoga before going home.
