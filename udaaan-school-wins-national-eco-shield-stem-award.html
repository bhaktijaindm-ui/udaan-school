Udaaan School Wins National "Eco-Shield" STEM Award

March 15, 2024 By Principal's Desk 4 Min Read

We are incredibly proud to announce that Udaaan School has officially secured the 1st place position at the Gurgaon Region Science Expo with our groundbreaking "Eco-Shield" Rainwater Harvesting model.

Our Grade 3 students spent over three months researching, designing, and eventually constructing a localized rainwater filtration system using pure STEM architecture principles.

1. The Idea Inception

During the heavy monsoons last July, our students noticed the massive amount of water being flushed into the city drainage systems without being utilized. Under the guidance of our science faculty, a dedicated problem-solving committee was formed entirely by primary-level students.

2. System Design & Physics

The "Eco-Shield" operates completely independent of electric pumps. Utilizing basic gravity-fed filtration metrics, the children built a multi-stage bio-filter utilizing coarse rocks, activated charcoal, and fine sand entirely sourced from local ecosystems.

3. National Recognition

The award is not just a trophy—it is a validation of experiential learning. The judging panel specifically praised the students' ability to explain the hydrostatic pressure concepts behind their model without relying on memorized scripts.

4. Future Implementation

Udaaan School will implement a scaled-up version of the children's design during the upcoming campus expansion, making the school actively water-positive during the monsoons.
